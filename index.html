Who We Are

Tiriji Hub Chess Club (THC²) is based at Kithoka Amani Children's Home (KACH) in Meru, under International Peace Initiatives (IPI). We coach children and young players, take them to rated tournaments, and run our own events to grow chess in the community.

Organiser and Chief Arbiter: Brian Karani, Candidate National Arbiter, Chess Kenya Federation.

Coaching

We run regular sessions for KACH residents and partner-school pupils, covering openings, tactics, tournament rules and handling, and match preparation. Our players have competed in FIDE-rated events against adult, internationally rated fields — and held their own.

Recent result — KeMU Internal FIDE Rated Tournament, 12 Sept 2026

Four THC² players competed in a 22-player, FIDE-rated field: Grace Naisoi finished 8th, Daniel Mutuma 10th, Favour Maua 15th, and Evans Mwiti 19th — all against opponents older, and mostly stronger, than themselves.
